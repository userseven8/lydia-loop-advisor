1 Stage 1: LoopKit Continuous System Identification ($R_{\text{gut}} = 0, \; \text{LoopKit Residuals}$)

Operating directly on LoopKit's exact Swift differential equations in Xcode (LoopAlgorithm.swift and InsulinMath.swift), system identification evaluates continuous glucose change across the full 14-day timeline during post-absorptive fasting:

$$\Delta\text{BG}(t \to t + H) = -\text{ISF} \cdot \sum_{i} \text{netBasalUnits}_i \cdot \Big[ F_{\text{expended}}(t + H - t_i) - F_{\text{expended}}(t - t_i) \Big] + \text{Drift}_{\text{basal}} \cdot H$$

Doses are accounted for via DoseEntry.netBasalUnits (boluses and temp basal deviations relative to scheduled basal) combined with Lyumjev exponential pharmacokinetic curves ($DIA = 360\text{ min}, \; t_{\text{peak}} = 55\text{ min}$). Minimizing prediction error residuals via continuous least squares across all unconfounded epochs isolates physical sensitivity across circadian phases without arbitrary event-slicing artifacts:

$$\mathbf{\min_{\text{ISF}, \text{Drift}} \sum_{k \in \text{Fasting}} \left( \Delta\text{BG}_{k} - \Big( -\text{ISF} \cdot \Delta I_{\text{net, metab}, k} + \text{Drift} \cdot H \Big) \right)^2}$$

2 Stage 2: Automated Circadian Basal Segmentation & Fasting-First Hierarchy

With ISF established, basal schedules are dynamically segmented and solved across distinct physiological time blocks directly from rolling data:

A. Automated Circadian Inflection & Change-Point Detection:

The algorithm evaluates 30-minute rolling bins of glucose velocity $\frac{d(\text{BG})}{dt}$ and resting flux $I_{\text{flux}}$ across 24 hours. Rather than assuming static clock hours, it dynamically discovers the biological transitions: early sleep nadir, dawn surge onset (cortisol/growth hormone rise), morning settling, daytime active metabolic phase, and bedtime wind-down.

B. Fasting-First Hierarchy (Tier 1 Priority: $R_{\text{gut}}=0$):

For all segments (including daytime), basal demand is solved first from unconfounded resting intervals ($R_{\text{gut}}=0$, no carbs for 2.5+ hours, $70 \le \text{BG} \le 180\text{ mg/dL}$, delivered rate $\le 0.35\text{ U/hr}$). The solved Stage 1 ISF converts residual glucose drift into true resting metabolic flux:

$$I_{\text{flux}}(t) = I_{\text{delivered}}(t) + \frac{\text{BG}(t_2) - \text{BG}(t_1)}{\text{ISF} \cdot (t_2 - t_1)}$$

This isolates true resting basal demand without post-prandial microbolus distortion.

C. Meal Mass-Balance Deconvolution Fallback (Tier 2):

If a daytime block has fewer than 3 clean resting hours due to frequent eating, the solver automatically falls back to linear meal mass-balance deconvolution across daytime meals ($I_{\text{tot}} - \frac{\Delta\text{BG}}{\text{ISF}} = \frac{1}{\text{CR}} \cdot \text{Carbs} + B_{\text{day}} \times \Delta t$), deriving basal from the zero-carb regression intercept.

D. Agglomerative Block Merging:

Adjacent discovered blocks whose solved rates are within the pump's minimum delivery resolution ($\le 0.025\text{ U/hr}$) are automatically merged into clean unified blocks, preventing unnecessary schedule complexity in Omnipod DASH.

3 Stage 3: Empirical Meal Clustering & Finite-Horizon Mass-Balance for Carb Ratios ($\text{CR}$)

Carb ratio boundaries are not hardcoded to arbitrary clock hours. On every execution, the app dynamically discovers Lydia's true eating patterns directly from her 14-day Nightscout meal treatments:

A. 1D Gaussian Kernel Density Estimation & Inter-Meal Valley Detection:

The app bins all meal timestamps across waking hours into 30-minute intervals and computes a Gaussian-smoothed density estimate:

$$S(i) = \frac{\sum_j B(j) \cdot \exp\left(-\frac{(i-j)^2}{2\sigma^2}\right)}{\sum_j \exp\left(-\frac{(i-j)^2}{2\sigma^2}\right)}, \quad \sigma = 1.2\text{ bins } (\approx 36\text{ min})$$

The algorithm scans for local density valleys (minima) between meal modes—the exact times of day when Lydia is least likely to eat. These valleys dynamically define the start times for Breakfast, Lunch, Afternoon Snack, Dinner, and Bedtime Snack (snapped to 30-minute steps for pump profile entry).

B. LoopKit Insulin Counteraction Effects (ICE) & Carb Sensitivity Factor ($CSF$):

Rather than assuming static clock windows or flat linear digestion, the app executes LoopKit's native 5-minute Insulin Counteraction Effects (ICE) algorithm across every CGM tick $[t_{k-1}, t_k]$:

$$\text{ICE}(t_k) = \underbrace{\big(\text{BG}(t_k) - \text{BG}(t_{k-1})\big)}_{\Delta\text{BG}_{\text{observed}}} - \underbrace{\big(-\Delta\text{IOB}_{\text{active}}(t_k) \cdot \text{ISF}\big)}_{\Delta\text{BG}_{\text{insulin expected}}}$$

By integrating $\text{ICE}(t)$ across the excursion horizon until counteraction plateaus, the algorithm measures the exact Total Gross Glycemic Surge ($\text{Total ICE}$ in mg/dL) produced by the meal:

$$\text{CSF} = \frac{\sum_{t} \text{ICE}(t)}{\text{Carbs}} \quad (\text{mg/dL of rise per gram of carbohydrate}), \qquad \mathbf{\text{CR} = \frac{\text{ISF}}{\text{CSF}}}$$
Pure Unconfounded LoopKit System Identification:
  • No Arbitrary Cutoffs: Evaluates meals according to their true biological absorption duration (30m fast, 180m standard, 300m slow/fat/protein), terminating when counteraction ends.
  • Zero Stacking Distortion: Unspent residual insulin ($\text{IOB}_{\text{end}}$) is never falsely credited as food insulin; the ICE equation isolates only insulin actually consumed counteracting carbohydrates.
  • Hypo Rescue Exclusion: Carbs consumed during hypoglycemia ($\text{BG} < 80\text{ mg/dL}$) are excluded because they arrest prior insulin overdose rather than requiring forward bolusing.
  • Physical Parameter Coupling: Carb Ratio is solved directly from the physical relationship $\text{CR} = \frac{\text{ISF}}{\text{CSF}}$, harmonizing insulin sensitivity and meal coverage across the entire circadian cycle.
