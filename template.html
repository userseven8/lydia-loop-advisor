Mathematical Methodology & 3-Stage Hierarchy Stage 1 → Stage 2 → Stage 3

Closed-form mass-balance equations solved in exact physical dependency order

1 Stage 1: Pharmacological Proof of ISF ($R_{\text{gut}} = 0, \; \text{BG}_{\text{bolus}} \ge 165\text{ mg/dL}$)

In automated insulin delivery (Loop), whenever a correction bolus is administered for hyperglycemia without food ($R_{\text{gut}} = 0$), Loop suspends basal delivery to $0.00\text{ U/hr}$. Therefore, the correction bolus $I_{\text{corr}}$ is the exclusive source of insulin driving the glucose drop down to nadir:

$$\mathbf{\text{ISF} = \frac{|\text{BG}_{\text{nadir}} - \text{BG}_{\text{bolus}}|}{I_{\text{corr}}}}$$

Because basal is suspended to zero during corrections, ISF is measured with zero confounding from basal or food. Evaluated dynamically across Lydia's history ($N = {{isf_proof_count}}$), empirical direct drops yield a median of {{isf_proof_median}} mg/dL/U (range: {{isf_proof_min}}–{{isf_proof_max}} mg/dL/U), establishing {{rec_isf}} mg/dL/U as her true physical sensitivity.

2 Stage 2: Multi-Block Basal Rates (Resting Flux & Meal Deconvolution)

With ISF established, basal rates are solved across distinct physiological time blocks without clamps or empirical TDD rules:

A. Nocturnal & Morning Resting Flux Equilibrium (`00:00`, `04:00`, `07:00`, `22:00`):

Evaluated over resting intervals with no carbs in 2.5h and stable glucose ($70 \le \text{BG} \le 180\text{ mg/dL}$). The solved Stage 1 ISF converts residual glucose drift into true resting metabolic flux:

$$I_{\text{flux}}(t) = I_{\text{delivered}}(t) + \frac{\text{BG}(t_2) - \text{BG}(t_1)}{\text{ISF} \cdot (t_2 - t_1)}$$

Sliding 30-minute sampling captures the dawn cortisol rise at `04:00` vs. deep sleep resting requirement at `00:00`.

B. Daytime Linear Meal Mass-Balance Deconvolution (`10:00`):

Because toddlers eat every 3 hours, pure daytime fasting does not exist. We solve daytime basal directly from the fundamental mass-balance of meals:

$$I_{\text{tot}} - \frac{\Delta\text{BG}}{\text{ISF}} = \frac{1}{\text{CR}} \cdot \text{Carbs} + \big(\mathbf{B_{\text{day}}} \times \Delta t\big)$$

A linear regression of $(I_{\text{net}})$ vs $(\text{Carbs})$ across all daytime meals reveals $B_{\text{day}}$ as the y-intercept (at $\text{Carbs} = 0$). This isolates awake baseline requirements directly from meal data with zero TDD rules.

3 Stage 3: Finite-Horizon Meal Mass-Balance for Carb Ratios ($\text{CR}$)

With $\text{ISF}$ solved in Stage 1 and $I_{\text{basal\_eq}}$ solved in Stage 2, the finite-horizon mass-balance equation integrates across complete meal excursions $[t_0, t_f]$:

$$\mathbf{\text{CR} = \frac{\text{Carbs}}{I_{\text{delivered}} - \int_{t_0}^{t_f} I_{\text{basal\_eq}}(t) \, dt + \frac{\text{BG}(t_f) - \text{BG}(t_0)}{\text{ISF}}}}$$
Strict Mathematical Hierarchy (No Feedback Confounding):

Carbohydrate ratios cannot be computed in isolation. To isolate pure food insulin, the equation explicitly deducts Stage 2 background basal ($\int I_{\text{basal\_eq}} dt$) and adjusts for residual glucose drift using Stage 1 sensitivity ($\Delta\text{BG}/\text{ISF}$). Solving in this exact 1 → 2 → 3 order eliminates parameter cross-talk and circular dependencies.
