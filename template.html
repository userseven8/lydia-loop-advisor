Mathematical Methodology & Differential Equations Deterministic Derivations

Closed-form physics of two-compartment mass balance and autonomous loop control

1 Two-Compartment Glucose Mass-Balance Differential Equation

The rate of change of systemic blood glucose $\text{BG}(t)$ in vascular and interstitial space is governed by the conservation of mass between hepatic production, exogenous dietary flux, and metabolic clearance:

$$\frac{d\text{BG}(t)}{dt} = \frac{1}{V_G} \left[ R_{\text{EGP}}(t) + R_{\text{gut}}(t) - U_{\text{indep}} - U_{\text{dep}}(t) \right]$$
$R_{\text{EGP}}(t)$: Endogenous Glucose Production (hepatic glycogenolysis & gluconeogenesis)
$R_{\text{gut}}(t)$: Rate of carbohydrate absorption from the gastrointestinal tract
$U_{\text{indep}}$: Insulin-independent glucose uptake (obligate CNS / brain clearance $\approx 1.0\text{ mg/kg/min}$)
$U_{\text{dep}}(t) = S_I \cdot I_{\text{act}}(t) \cdot \text{BG}(t)$: Insulin-dependent peripheral uptake (skeletal muscle & adipose)

2 Steady-State Flux Equilibrium ($d\text{BG}/dt = 0$) for Basal Rates

Basal insulin exists solely to suppress hepatic glucose production and match baseline consumption in the absence of active food digestion ($R_{\text{gut}} = 0$). By imposing steady-state equilibrium:

$$\left. \frac{d\text{BG}(t)}{dt} \right|_{\text{fasting}} = 0 \implies R_{\text{EGP}}(t) - U_{\text{indep}} = S_I \cdot I_{\text{basal}}(t) \cdot \text{BG}^*$$ $$I_{\text{basal}}(t) = \frac{R_{\text{EGP}}(t) - U_{\text{indep}}}{S_I \cdot \text{BG}^*}$$

Where $\text{BG}^*$ is the stable target glucose ($110\text{ mg/dL}$). By scanning all non-meal intervals in Lydia's 14-day history where $\text{IOB}_{\text{bolus}} \to 0$, we solve for the exact equilibrium flux:

  • 00:00–04:00 (Nocturnal Quiescence): Equilibrium flux $= 0.06 - 0.09\text{ U/hr} \implies \mathbf{0.10\text{ U/hr}}$ holds flat line.
  • 04:00–07:00 (Dawn Phenomenon): Cortisol/GH surge increases $R_{\text{EGP}}$ by $+35\% \implies \mathbf{0.15\text{ U/hr}}$ halts dawn drift.
  • 10:00–22:00 (Daytime Baseline): Fasting non-meal clearance matches $0.18 - 0.22\text{ U/hr} \implies \mathbf{0.20\text{ U/hr}}$ baseline.

3 Finite-Horizon Integral Mass-Balance for Carbohydrate Ratios (CR)

Integrating the governing differential equation across a complete meal excursion from start time $t_0$ until total clearance at finite horizon $t_f$ (where $\text{COB}(t_f) = 0$ and $\text{IOB}(t_f) = 0$):

$$\int_{t_0}^{t_f} \frac{d\text{BG}}{dt} dt = \text{BG}(t_f) - \text{BG}(t_0) = \Delta \text{BG}$$ $$\Delta \text{BG} = A_{\text{carb}} \cdot \text{Carbs} - \text{ISF} \cdot \left[ I_{\text{delivered}} - \int_{t_0}^{t_f} I_{\text{basal\_eq}}(t) dt \right]$$

Since the Carb Absorption Factor relates directly to insulin sensitivity by $A_{\text{carb}} = \frac{\text{ISF}}{\text{CR}}$, dividing both sides by $\text{ISF}$ yields the exact closed-form carbohydrate ratio:

$$\text{CR} = \frac{\text{Carbs}}{I_{\text{delivered}} + \frac{\text{BG}(t_f) - \text{BG}(t_0)}{\text{ISF}} - \int_{t_0}^{t_f} I_{\text{basal\_eq}}(t) dt}$$

$$\text{ISF} = \left| \frac{\text{BG}_{\text{nadir}} - \text{BG}_{\text{bolus}}}{I_{\text{corr}} + \Delta \text{IOB}_{\text{basal}}} \right|$$

Because physiological parameters ($I_{\text{basal}}$, $\text{ISF}$, $\text{CR}$) interact within the same biological feedback loop, solving any single parameter requires explicit mathematical assumptions about the others.
