1 Stage 1: Empirical ISF Measurement & Anti-Stacking Safety

To measure Lydia's true biological response to Lyumjev, we isolate nighttime correction events occurring with zero active carbohydrates ($R_{\text{gut}} = 0$) and no active overrides (illness, exercise, failing pod). We measure the drop from pre-bolus glucose to post-correction nadir:

$$\mathbf{\text{ISF}_{\text{physio}} = \text{median}\left(\frac{|\text{BG}_{\text{nadir}} - \text{BG}_{\text{bolus}}|}{I_{\text{delivered}}}\right) = {{rec_isf}}\text{ mg/dL/U}}$$

Physiological Equilibrium & Opposing Error Cancellation:

During an isolated correction window, the complete blood glucose balance equation is:

$$\Delta\text{BG} = - \text{ISF} \cdot I_{\text{corr}} - \underbrace{\text{ISF} \cdot I_{\text{basal}}}_{\text{Deflates BG}} + \underbrace{\text{EGP}_{\text{liver}}}_{\text{Inflates BG}}$$

During resting periods without meals, basal delivery roughly matches endogenous hepatic glucose production ($\text{EGP}_{\text{liver}} \approx \text{ISF} \cdot I_{\text{basal}}$). The two unmodeled terms neutralize each other, leaving the marginal drop directly proportional to the correction bolus:

$$\Delta\text{BG} \approx - \text{ISF} \cdot I_{\text{corr}} \iff \text{ISF} \approx \frac{|\Delta\text{BG}|}{I_{\text{corr}}}$$

This biological equilibrium preserves stability: undercounting insulin and omitting liver production push in opposite directions and cancel, accurately isolating the net marginal sensitivity.

2 Stage 2: Decoupled Basal Identification (Fasting Mass-Balance Equilibrium & Activity Variance)

During resting periods without active meal digestion ($R_{\text{gut}} = 0$), blood glucose homeostasis is maintained by matching pump delivery (scheduled basal + automated micro-boluses) to endogenous hepatic glucose output:

$$B_{\text{basal}} = \frac{\int r_{\text{pump}}(t) \, dt}{\Delta t} + \frac{\Delta\text{BG}}{\text{ISF}_{\text{physio}} \cdot \Delta t}$$

Basal rates are isolated directly from clean fasting intervals without circular coupling to meal ratios. Two specialized physiological safeguards protect baseline accuracy:

  • Postprandial Contamination Guard (Bedtime/Overnight): Detects bimodal dinner digestion spilling into 22:00–00:00, separating clean fasting nights (0.01 U/hr) from unabsorbed protein/fat spikes (up to 0.75 U/hr) to prevent nocturnal over-basaling.

The solver isolates quiet resting homeostasis (0.10 U/hr) so daytime basal is not erroneously downgraded by exercise-depressed median flux, preventing creeping glucose rises during sedentary rest.

3 Stage 3: Settled Target Meal Carb Ratio Regression & Diagnostic Quality Flags

Using the independently measured resting basal baseline, each 3-hour post-meal window determines the net target meal insulin required to return glucose flat to baseline ($\Delta\text{BG} = 0$):

$$I_{\text{meal}}^{\text{target}} = I_{\text{delivered}} - (B_{\text{resting}} \times 3\text{h}) + \frac{\Delta\text{BG}}{\text{ISF}_{\text{physio}}}$$

To prevent severe distortion from acute glucotoxicity spikes ($+112, +165\text{ mg/dL}$) or over-bolused rebound crashes ($-90\text{ mg/dL}$), Carb Ratios are fitted via Settled Target Linear Regression ($|\Delta\text{BG}| \le 60\text{ mg/dL}$), finding the true upfront ratio needed for flat postprandial return ($\text{CR} = \sum C_i^2 / \sum C_i I_{\text{meal}, i}^{\text{target}}$):

$$\text{SE}(\hat{\beta}) = \sqrt{\frac{\sum (I_i - \hat{\beta} C_i)^2}{(n-1)\sum C_i^2}}, \quad \text{CI}_{95\%} = \left[ \frac{1}{\hat{\beta} + t_{0.975} \cdot \text{SE}(\hat{\beta})}, \; \frac{1}{\hat{\beta} - t_{0.975} \cdot \text{SE}(\hat{\beta})} \right]$$

Statistical Uncertainty & Fat/Protein Guard: High-fat or high-protein meals (such as complex dinners) delay gastric emptying and induce late Loop micro-boluses that artificially deflate the raw point estimate. Providing both the 95% Confidence Interval and observed empirical range ensures that active profile settings falling safely within the upper CI boundary (such as 1:9.0–1:10.0 at dinner) are recognized as validated, preventing excessive upfront insulin and protecting bedtime glycemic stability.
