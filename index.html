The Closed-Loop Feedback Dilemma

In open loop (syringes), using Sp ≈ 150–180 mg/dL/U works because no automation intervenes. In closed loop, subcutaneous insulin has an irreversible 20-minute transport delay:

  • Loop’s Retrospective Correction (RC) acts as an Integrator (I).
  • During the initial flat 20 minutes, RC assumes an unannounced carb spike and piles on microboluses (Integral Windup).
  • Entering raw Sp directly into Loop leads to delayed insulin stacking crashes.
