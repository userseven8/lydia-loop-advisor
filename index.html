Closed-form mass-balance equations solved in exact physical dependency order

1 Stage 1: Steady-State Basal Equilibrium ($R_{\text{gut}} = 0, \; d\text{BG}/dt \approx 0$)

Basal insulin exists exclusively to match endogenous hepatic glucose output in the absence of food absorption ($R_{\text{gut}} = 0$). Over fasting steady-state intervals $[t_1, t_2]$:

$$I_{\text{basal\_eq}}(t) = I_{\text{delivered}}(t) + \frac{\text{BG}(t_2) - \text{BG}(t_1)}{\text{ISF} \cdot (t_2 - t_1)}$$

When glucose holds flat ($\Delta\text{BG} \to 0$), the drift term vanishes, yielding $I_{\text{basal\_eq}} = I_{\text{delivered}}$, perfectly decoupled from food and independent of Carb Ratios.

2 Stage 2: Pharmacological Proof of ISF ($R_{\text{gut}} = 0, \; \text{BG}_{\text{bolus}} \ge 165\text{ mg/dL}$)

With background basal calibrated, insulin sensitivity is solved from unconfounded hyperglycemic excursions where no food intake occurs ($R_{\text{gut}} = 0$), integrated from bolus delivery to the glycemic nadir:

$$\text{ISF} = \frac{|\text{BG}_{\text{nadir}} - \text{BG}_{\text{bolus}}|}{I_{\text{corr}} + \Delta\text{IOB}_{\text{basal}}}$$

Where $I_{\text{corr}}$ is the correction dose and $\Delta\text{IOB}_{\text{basal}} = \int_{t_{\text{bolus}}}^{t_{\text{nadir}}} (B_{\text{temp}}(t) - B_{\text{sched}}(t)) \, dt$ accounts for Loop basal modulation during the drop. Evaluated across Lydia's history ($N = 5$), empirical direct drops yield a median of 270.0 mg/dL/U (range: 204–320 mg/dL/U), validating the active setting of 210 mg/dL/U as the safe lower bound.

3 Stage 3: Finite-Horizon Meal Mass-Balance for Carb Ratios ($\text{CR}$)

With $I_{\text{basal\_eq}}$ solved in Stage 1 and $\text{ISF}$ solved in Stage 2, the finite-horizon mass-balance equation integrates across a complete meal excursion $[t_0, t_f]$:

$$\Delta\text{BG} = A_{\text{carb}} \cdot \text{Carbs} - \text{ISF} \cdot \left[ I_{\text{delivered}} - \int_{t_0}^{t_f} I_{\text{basal\_eq}}(t) \, dt \right]$$

Substituting the biophysical identity $A_{\text{carb}} = \frac{\text{ISF}}{\text{CR}}$ directly into this mass-balance equation yields the exact closed-form carbohydrate ratio:

$$\mathbf{\text{CR} = \frac{\text{Carbs}}{I_{\text{delivered}} - \int_{t_0}^{t_f} I_{\text{basal\_eq}}(t) \, dt + \frac{\text{BG}(t_f) - \text{BG}(t_0)}{\text{ISF}}}}$$
Strict Mathematical Hierarchy (No Feedback Confounding):

Carbohydrate ratios cannot be computed in isolation. To isolate pure food insulin, the equation explicitly deducts Stage 1 background basal ($\int I_{\text{basal\_eq}} dt$) and adjusts for residual glucose drift using Stage 2 sensitivity ($\Delta\text{BG}/\text{ISF}$). Solving in this exact 1 → 2 → 3 order eliminates parameter cross-talk and circular dependencies.
