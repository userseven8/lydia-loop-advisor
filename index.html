Why Damping is Applied to Everything

Control Theory Damping

Loop runs an automated feedback controller that delivers microboluses every 5 minutes. Because subcutaneous Lyumjev takes 45 minutes to reach peak activity, the physical plant has an unavoidable phase delay. If settings are tuned to raw physical sensitivity, Loop aggressively stacks insulin before glucose drops, triggering limit cycles:

1. Damped ISF (240)
Plant $S_p \approx 140$ is damped by $1.6\times$ ($GM \ge 2.5$) so Loop correction pulses stay small and avoid 2-hour rebound crashes.
2. Damped Basal (0.05/0.10)
The pump cannot suck insulin back out ($u \ge 0$). Keeping basal low guarantees downward braking margin ($GM_{\text{downward}} \ge 3.4$) when glucose falls.
3. Closed-Loop CR PEM
Meal ratios are solved directly via Prediction Error Method deconvolution without artificial clamps, tracking true carbohydrate clearance.
